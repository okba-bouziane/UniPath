## Programmes

### International Foundation Year (IFY)
A three-term foundation programme leading to undergraduate degrees. Streams: Business & Finance, Law, Engineering, Art & Design.

### International Year One (IY1)
Business, Economics and Computing routes. Successful students progress to Year 2 of the degree.

### Pre-Master's Programme
One or two terms depending on English level, leading to master's degrees in Business, Finance, Data Science and Law.

## Entry requirements

- **IFY:** Algerian Baccalauréat with an overall average of at least 13/20.
- **IY1:** Algerian Baccalauréat with at least 14/20 and one completed year of higher education.
- **Pre-Master's:** Licence with an average of at least 12/20. Work experience can be considered for business routes.

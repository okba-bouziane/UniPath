## Programmes

### International Foundation Year (IFY)
A three-term programme in Business, Computing, Engineering and Health Sciences.

### International Year One (IY1)
Business Management and Computing routes leading to Year 2 of the degree.

### Pre-Master's Programme
A one-term to three-term programme depending on English level, leading to master's degrees in Business, Computing and Public Health.

## Entry requirements

- **IFY:** Algerian Baccalauréat with an overall average of at least 11/20.
- **IY1:** Algerian Baccalauréat with at least 13/20, plus one year of university study.
- **Pre-Master's:** Licence with an average of at least 10/20.

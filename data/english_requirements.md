# English Language Requirements (IELTS)

> **SAMPLE DATA** — simplified guidance for demo purposes only. Always confirm with the university and the official UKVI rules.

## Which test?
For a Student visa to study a pathway programme below degree level (for example an International Foundation Year), students usually need a **UKVI-approved Secure English Language Test (SELT)**, such as **IELTS for UKVI (Academic)**. For degree-level study, many universities can assess English themselves, but IELTS for UKVI is the safest choice.

## Scores
IELTS scores are given as an **overall band** plus a score for each of the four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Universities set both a minimum overall score and a minimum score in each skill.

## IELTS in Algeria
IELTS for UKVI can be taken at test centres in Algiers and Oran (sample data). Results are typically available after 13 days for the paper test and 3–5 days for the computer test (sample data).

## If your score is too low
Most pathway providers offer **pre-sessional English** courses of 10 to 30 weeks before the main programme starts. A sample pre-sessional fee is **£2,300 per 10 weeks**.

## Validity
IELTS results are normally valid for **2 years** from the test date.

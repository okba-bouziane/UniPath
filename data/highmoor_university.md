## Programmes

### International Foundation Year (IFY)
A one-year programme with streams in Engineering, Physical Sciences, Business and Social Sciences. Note: Scottish undergraduate degrees usually last four years.

### International Year One (IY1)
Engineering and Business routes. Successful students progress to Year 2 of a four-year Scottish degree.

### Pre-Master's Programme
A two-term programme with progression to master's degrees in Engineering, Business and Data Science.

## Entry requirements

- **IFY:** Algerian Baccalauréat with an overall average of at least 12/20, and at least 12/20 in Mathematics for Engineering and Science streams.
- **IY1:** Algerian Baccalauréat with at least 15/20.
- **Pre-Master's:** Licence with an average of at least 12/20.

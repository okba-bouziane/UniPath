# UK Student Visa — Overview for Algerian Students

> **SAMPLE DATA** — simplified overview with made-up values for demo purposes only. Always check the official GOV.UK guidance before applying.

## Steps
1. Receive an unconditional offer and pay the tuition deposit.
2. Receive a **CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies)** number from the university.
3. Apply online for the Student visa and pay the visa fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS).
4. Book a biometrics appointment at the visa application centre in Algiers (sample data).
5. Receive a decision, usually within 3 weeks (sample data).

## Sample costs
- **Visa application fee:** £520 (sample data)
- **Immigration Health Surcharge:** £780 per year of study (sample data)

## Financial requirement
Students must show they can pay the remaining first-year tuition fees plus living costs. Sample monthly living cost amounts:
- **London:** £1,480 per month, for up to 9 months
- **Outside London:** £1,140 per month, for up to 9 months

The money must have been held in the bank account for at least **28 consecutive days**.

## Tuberculosis test
Applicants from Algeria need a **TB test certificate** from an approved clinic (sample data).

# Scholarships and Living Costs

> **SAMPLE DATA** — fictional awards and made-up values for demo purposes only.

## Scholarships for pathway students
- **Riverton University — Algeria Merit Award:** £2,000 off tuition for a Baccalauréat average of 15/20 or higher.
- **Northbridge University — Early Payment Discount:** £1,000 off tuition if the full fee is paid before 1 June.
- **Kingsford University London — Pathway Excellence Scholarship:** up to £3,000 for students who progress with a high final grade (awarded in the degree year).
- University of Eastmere and Highmoor University do not list pathway-specific scholarships in this sample data.

## Working while studying
Students on a pathway programme below degree level are usually allowed to work **up to 10 hours per week** during term time; degree-level students up to 20 hours (sample data).

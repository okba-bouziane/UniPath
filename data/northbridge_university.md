## Programmes

### International Foundation Year (IFY)
A one-year programme (3 terms) that prepares students who have completed the Algerian Baccalauréat for first-year undergraduate study. Streams: Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Life Sciences.

### International Year One (IY1)
Equivalent to the first year of a bachelor's degree. Students who pass progress directly to Year 2 at Northbridge University. Available in Business and Computer Science.

### Pre-Master's Programme
A two-term programme for graduates (Licence holders) who need academic and English preparation before a master's degree.

## Entry requirements

- **IFY:** Algerian Baccalauréat with an overall average of at least 12/20.
- **IY1:** Algerian Baccalauréat with at least 14/20, plus one year of university study in Algeria with an average of 11/20 or higher.
- **Pre-Master's:** Licence (3-year bachelor's) with an average of at least 11/20.

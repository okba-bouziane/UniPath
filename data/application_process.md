# How to Apply to a UK Pathway Programme

> **SAMPLE DATA** — general guidance for demo purposes only.

## Where to apply
Pathway programmes are usually applied for **directly through the pathway provider's online application**, not through UCAS. Students can apply on their own or through an approved education agent.

## Documents checklist
- Passport (valid for the full duration of the course)
- Baccalauréat certificate and transcript (relevé de notes), with certified English translations
- University transcripts and Licence certificate (for IY1, Pre-Master's or Graduate Diploma)
- IELTS for UKVI result, if available (a conditional offer can be issued without it)
- Personal statement (around 500 words)
- One academic reference letter

## Timeline
1. Apply 4–6 months before the intake.
2. Receive a conditional or unconditional offer, usually within 2 weeks (sample data).
3. Meet any conditions (for example the IELTS score) and pay the deposit.
4. Receive the CAS and apply for the Student visa.

## Tips
- Applying early gives more time for the visa process and accommodation booking.
- Deadlines are different for each university and intake.

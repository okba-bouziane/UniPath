# Algerian Baccalauréat and UK Pathway Entry

> **SAMPLE DATA** — simplified guidance with made-up values for demo purposes only. Always confirm with the university.

## Why a pathway?
The Algerian Baccalauréat is generally not accepted for direct entry to Year 1 of most UK bachelor's degrees, because UK degrees expect 13 years of schooling (A-levels or equivalent). A one-year International Foundation Year (IFY) closes this gap.

## Which pathway fits which student?

| Your situation | Typical pathway |
|---|---|
| Just finished the Baccalauréat | International Foundation Year |
| Baccalauréat + one year of university in Algeria | International Year One |
| Licence (3-year degree) holder, wants a master's | Pre-Master's Programme |
| Licence in a different subject from the planned master's | Graduate Diploma |

## Grades
Universities usually look at the **overall Baccalauréat average (moyenne générale) out of 20**. Some Engineering and Science streams also require a minimum grade in Mathematics.

## Documents
Students need certified English translations of the Baccalauréat certificate and transcript (relevé de notes).
